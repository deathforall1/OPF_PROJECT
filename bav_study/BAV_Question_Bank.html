The +4/−1 Drill

Seventy-five multiple-choice questions written in Prof. Mohanty's idiom — the traps, the consistency checks and the short numericals he actually sets. A predicted 25-question interim paper under +4/−1 marking, and a 57-question end-term bank sorted by topic. Every question carries a full worked solution.

How this paper is built

Twenty-five questions, +4 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong one, maximum 100. The weighting follows the course: roughly nine questions on the post-Quiz-3 block (venture capital, private companies, real options, practical valuation), five on the quantitative material he has said he will examine, and the rest spread across the three quizzes.

Under this marking a blind guess among four options has an expected value of 0.25 × 4 − 0.75 × 1 = +0.25, so guessing is never negative-EV — and eliminating even one option makes it clearly worth answering. Leave a question blank only if you cannot eliminate anything at all.

How to use the bank

Fifty-seven questions grouped by topic. Filter to the block you are revising, answer without looking, then read the solution even when you were right — several solutions carry a second point that the question itself does not reach.
